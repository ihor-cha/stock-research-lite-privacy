Privacy Policy — Stock Research Lite

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Stock Research Lite is an educational stock-research workflow for ChatGPT. It helps users research public companies and understand valuation and financial metrics such as EV/EBITDA, EBITDA, net income, net debt, comparable companies, and stock developments.

1. Data collected by Stock Research Lite

Stock Research Lite is a skills-only ChatGPT plugin and does not operate its own external server, database, user account system, advertising system, analytics service, or payment system.

Stock Research Lite does not independently collect or retain personal data from users. Users should not provide passwords, API keys, payment-card information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information to the plugin.

2. How information is used

The information a user provides in a ChatGPT conversation is used to generate the requested stock-research and educational response. Stock Research Lite is designed to minimize information collection and to focus on the company or financial topic requested by the user.

3. Third parties and data recipients

Stock Research Lite does not independently transmit user information to a separate third-party service. Because the plugin operates within ChatGPT, information submitted to it is processed by OpenAI as part of the ChatGPT service. OpenAI's handling of personal data is governed by OpenAI's applicable privacy policy and terms.

Stock Research Lite may instruct ChatGPT to perform web research using publicly available sources. The plugin itself does not maintain a separate copy of those sources or create an independent user profile.

4. Data retention

Stock Research Lite itself does not maintain a separate database or retention system for user data. Any retention or deletion of ChatGPT conversation data is governed by the applicable OpenAI product settings, policies, and terms.

5. User controls

Users can control their use of Stock Research Lite by choosing what information they provide in ChatGPT and by using the privacy, data, and conversation controls available in their ChatGPT account. Users should consult OpenAI's current privacy documentation for details about those controls.

6. Children's privacy

Stock Research Lite is intended for users who are permitted to use ChatGPT under OpenAI's applicable age requirements. The plugin is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the functionality of Stock Research Lite changes or when applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

8. Contact

Privacy questions concerning Stock Research Lite may be directed to the developer through the contact information provided with the Stock Research Lite plugin listing.

9. OpenAI privacy information

For information about how OpenAI handles data in ChatGPT, please refer to OpenAI's current privacy policy and applicable ChatGPT data controls.

Stock Research Lite is an educational research tool and does not provide personalized investment advice.
